# Pip and the Tiny Power Helpers

Ten-page interactive English storybook for children age 5, produced from `02_storybook_script/fusion-ready-semiconductors-storyboard.md`.

## Open the storybook

Open `index.html` in a browser.

- Arrow buttons, page dots, keyboard arrows, and touch swipes change pages.
- The sparkle button reveals three clickable areas on every page.
- Clicking a story detail plays a short stepped paper-spark animation and a small sound.
- The music button toggles sound.
- The info button opens a grown-up accuracy note for the current page.
- Story text is HTML and is not baked into the illustrations.

## Production contents

- `index.html` — finished interactive reader.
- `page-01/background.png` through `page-10/background.png` — final 1536 × 1024 paper-collage page artwork.
- `source-storyboard.md` — production source and layer plan.

## Accuracy boundary

The story describes a letter of intent and proposed research support. It does not claim that the full award has been received or spent, that the electronics have succeeded, or that commercial fusion electricity is ready.

## Image generation

The ten page illustrations were generated with the built-in image generation workflow using the supplied Pip, Dot, and Fenn character boards as strict design references. All prompts required a 3:2 paper-collage page, an irregular lower text area, no baked-in words, and no claim of completed fusion power.
