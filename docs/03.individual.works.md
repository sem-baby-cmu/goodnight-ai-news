## **4\. Problem Statement**

**DRAFT from Deokhwa Jeong**

**The problem:** Children ages 5 to 10 are growing up with AI, but AI news is written for adults and often mixes hype with fact. Parents want to explain it, yet have no trusted, simple source.

**Our question:** Can an agentic AI system turn real AI news into accurate, age appropriate stories that parents can verify and children look forward to every night?

**Our contribution:** A multi-agent pipeline that filters AI news and checks each claim against its source. Weak or conflicting stories go to parents for review, and approved ones become a bedtime story with one AI concept to learn.

**Why it matters:** Kids news apps rarely cover AI, and AI newsletters are made for adults. Our innovation: parents can see the source behind each story.

**Our impact:** AI literate children, and parents who can talk about AI with confidence. 

**DRAFT from Jinyu Liu**

**The problem:** Tech news moves rapidly and contains hype, making it difficult for parents to extract accurate, educational content to teach their kids about AI. Existing news platforms are built for adults and lack child-friendly, interactive formats.

**Our question:** Can an automated multi-agent pipeline transform daily AI news trends into human-reviewed, interactive storybooks with read-aloud narration and quizzes for children ages 4–8?

**Our contribution:** A storybook generator and lightweight reader engine that turns verified news into self-contained interactive packages featuring tap-to-explore illustrations, synchronized text highlighting, and picture quizzes.

**Why it matters:** Current children's news apps ignore AI developments while adult tech trackers offer no child-safe adaptation. We bridge trend tracking with early childhood AI literacy through cross-platform interactive media.

**Our impact:** Empowers young children to build foundational AI literacy and critical thinking through engaging, verified daily stories accessible on any mobile device.

**DRAFT from Judy Lee**

**The problem:** AI is shaping the world our children will grow up in, yet AI news is written for adults. Parents of kids ages 5 to 10 want to raise AI literate children, but lack simple, safe ways to explain it.

**The problem:** AI is shaping the world our children will grow up in, but AI news is written for adults. Parents of kids ages 5 to 10 want to raise AI-literate children, yet they’re often too busy or too tired at bedtime to find, fact-check, and explain the news themselves.
