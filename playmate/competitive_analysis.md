## Key Findings for Goodnight, AI

### 1. Quizzes
- **Khan Academy Kids** uses no-wrong-answer tap games for under-7s — matches our Story Scene Tap design.
- **Duolingo ABC** and **Epic!** use multiple-choice for older kids — aligns with our True or False Zapper.
- **Finding:** picture-first for ages 5–7, text-based for 8–10 is the industry norm. We are on track.

### 2. Streaks
- **Duolingo** streak = most powerful retention mechanic in kids apps; losing a streak causes frustration, so apps offer "streak shields."
- **Khan Academy** uses softer "daily mission" framing to avoid anxiety in young kids.
- **Finding:** Keep streaks for ages 8–10; for ages 5–7, show a friendly moon counter ("5 nights") not a number-at-risk streak.

### 3. Badges
- **Epic!** gives certificates at reading milestones (10, 25, 50 books) — parents print them out.
- **Khan Academy** gives character rewards (new outfits for in-app animals).
- **Finding:** Real-world reward suggestions (park trip, movie night) differentiate Goodnight, AI from all five apps. No app reviewed does this.

### 4. Screen Time
- Only **Epic!** offers a parental time limit (via parent dashboard).
- None of the five apps show an in-story timer to the child.
- **Finding:** Our 10-minute in-story moon timer is unique and matches AAP guidelines (1 hour/day for ages 6+). This is a clear differentiator.

### 5. COPPA / Data
- **Khan Academy Kids** and **Duolingo ABC** require no account for children; progress tied to device.
- **Epic!** requires a parent email.
- **Finding:** Our "no account, localStorage only" approach matches the best-in-class standard.

### 6. AI Content
- None of the five apps cover AI news.
- **Newsela** covers current events but requires school accounts and has no gamification.
- **Finding:** Confirmed: no competitor serves AI news for ages 5–10 with gamification. This is our niche.
